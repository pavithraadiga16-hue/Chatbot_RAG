NORTHSTAR INSTITUTE — ACADEMIC CALENDAR GUIDANCE (DUMMY DEVELOPMENT SAMPLE)

This fictional calendar guidance is sample content for PhinsIQ development and testing only. It is not an official calendar and must be replaced with the institution's approved dates before use.

The Registrar publishes the academic calendar before the start of each academic year. It lists semester start and end dates, registration windows, teaching days, examination periods, institute holidays, and grade publication windows.

Dates in a draft calendar may change. Students should rely on the latest calendar shared through the official student portal and institute email. Department-specific events and examination timetables may be published separately.

For a calendar correction, students should contact the Registrar's Office and include the semester and programme concerned.
