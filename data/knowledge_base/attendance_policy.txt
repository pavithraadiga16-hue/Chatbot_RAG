NORTHSTAR INSTITUTE — ATTENDANCE POLICY (DUMMY DEVELOPMENT SAMPLE)

This fictional policy is sample content for PhinsIQ development and testing only. It is not an official policy and must be replaced with institution-approved text before use.

Students are expected to maintain at least 75% attendance in each subject to be eligible to appear for end-semester examinations. Attendance is calculated subject-wise from the classes scheduled for that subject.

Students whose attendance is between 65% and 74% may apply for condonation when there are documented exceptional circumstances. Condonation is reviewed by the academic office and is not automatic. Students below 65% are normally not eligible to sit the examination unless an exception is approved under the institution's rules.

Students should report attendance record errors to the course instructor within five working days of the relevant class. Medical or other supporting documents may be requested for an attendance appeal.
