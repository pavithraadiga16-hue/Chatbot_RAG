NORTHSTAR INSTITUTE — EXAMINATION RULES (DUMMY DEVELOPMENT SAMPLE)

This fictional policy is sample content for PhinsIQ development and testing only. It is not an official policy and must be replaced with institution-approved text before use.

Students must carry their institute identity card and examination admit card to each examination. Students should arrive at least fifteen minutes before the published start time. Entry may be refused after thirty minutes unless the chief superintendent approves an exception.

Mobile phones, smart watches, notes, and other unauthorized materials must be switched off and stored as directed by examination staff. Calculators may be used only when the examination instructions permit them.

Students who miss an examination because of a documented emergency should notify the examination office within two working days and submit supporting documents. A make-up examination is subject to review and approval; it is not guaranteed.
