Scholarship eligibility depends on the award. The sample merit award requires a minimum 8.0 CGPA in the previous academic year and no unresolved academic-integrity case. The sample need-based award considers household income and demonstrated financial need. Meeting minimum criteria does not guarantee an award because funds are limited.

Applicants should submit the completed scholarship form, current institute identity card, previous-year marksheet, proof of admission or enrollment, and a signed fee statement. Need-based applicants should also provide a current income certificate and any supporting documents requested by the scholarship office.

Applications are submitted to the Student Support Office by the date announced for that academic year. Incomplete applications may be returned for correction. Award decisions are communicated through the student's institute email account.
