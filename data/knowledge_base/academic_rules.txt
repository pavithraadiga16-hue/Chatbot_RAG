NORTHSTAR INSTITUTE — ACADEMIC RULES (DUMMY DEVELOPMENT SAMPLE)

This fictional policy is sample content for PhinsIQ development and testing only. It is not an official policy and must be replaced with institution-approved text before use.

Students normally register for the courses listed for their programme and semester. Add or drop requests must be submitted to the academic office during the first two weeks of the semester, subject to adviser approval and seat availability.

To progress to the next academic year, a student must meet the programme's credit requirements and clear any mandatory courses specified by the department. The registrar publishes programme-specific progression requirements before each academic year.

Academic integrity is expected in all coursework. Plagiarism, unauthorized collaboration, impersonation, or falsification of academic records may be referred to the Academic Integrity Committee. Students may submit a written appeal within ten working days of receiving a decision.
